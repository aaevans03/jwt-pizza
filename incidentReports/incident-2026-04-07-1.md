# Incident: 2026-04-07 16:14:01

## Summary

Between 16:14 UTC and 16:30 UTC on 2026.04.07, 100% of users encountered the inability to order pizzas from the JWT Pizza Factory.

The event was triggered by the chaos monkey at 16:14, from my initialization. The pizza factory failed to fulfill customer orders for 16 minutes.

After checking my Grafana dashboard, I look through the graphs and logs and followed a link to resolve the problem. This HIGH severity incident affected 8 users.

Better Grafana alerts will be put in place to detect incidents in a more timely matter.

## Detection

This incident was detected when I checked my JWT Pizza metrics dashboard and noticed that all pizza purchase requests were failing.

I did not realize that my Grafana alerts were not set up correctly, so I expected a notification, but I did not get one. I could have responded faster if I had received a notification, since I was not busy at the time of alert.

Now that I know what a system breakage looks like, I will set up more specific alerts in Grafana so that a problem can be immediately detected.

## Impact

For 16 minutes between 16:14 UTC and 16:30 UTC on 2026.04.07, the pizza factory failed to fulfill orders.

This incident affected 8 active users on the app, who experienced an inability to have their orders fulfilled.

No support tickets or social media posts were submitted.

## Timeline

All times are UTC.

- *2:30* - Chaos testing initiated
- *16:14* - Pizza factory started failing to fulfill orders.
- *16:29* - Grafana JWT Pizza metrics dashboard opened.
- *16:30* - Link followed in failed pizza order response log to end chaos.
- *16:30* - Issue resolved, ordering service working as normal.

## Response

After checking my Grafana JWT Pizza dashboard at 16:29 UTC, I was aware of the problem. I did not receive an email notification.

After I scanned the graphs and logs, I noticed pizza order fulfillment was down. I followed the chaos monkey link in logs that restored the service.

## Root cause

The root cause of the incident was a blocking of pizza ordering from the JWT Pizza Factory, due to a chaos monkey. Requests were being sent from JWT Pizza Service to the factory, but it rejected all orders, returning a message that said "chaos monkey".

Since this was an internally planned incident, there is no fix besides shortening the response time.

## Resolution

The service was restored when I found a log with a failed pizza order response JSON. In it was a link to the JWT Pizza Factory site to restore normal ordering. Once I clicked it, the service was all normal, and pizza ordering resumed as normal.

To improve time to mitigation, setting up proper detection of errors is needed.

## Prevention

No other real incidents have the same root cause. All incidents resulting from initiated chaos testing do.
